Plant Health Tips Agronomic Best Practices
🔍 Early Morning Inspection

Inspect lower canopy leaves during early daylight. Fungal spores and early lesions first appear on shaded, humid leaf undersides.

💧 Drip & Base Irrigation

Water at the root zone rather than overhead sprinkling. Prolonged foliar wetness exceeding 6 hours is the primary trigger for bacterial blight and rust.

✂️ Sanitation & Tool Sterilization

Prune diseased foliage and safely dispose away from compost. Disinfect pruners with 70% isopropyl alcohol between plants to halt cross-contamination.

Pesticide Recommendation Guide

Indian CIBRC & ICAR Agronomic Standards

PlantCare AI references registered active ingredients approved by the Central Insecticides Board & Registration Committee (CIBRC) and ICAR extension packages. Chemical dosages are only recommended for supported foliar diseases:

Tomato & Potato Early Blight Fungal
Active: Mancozeb 75% WP @ 2.0 g/L or Azoxystrobin 23% SC @ 1.0 ml/L
Waiting Period (PHI): 7 days. Spray at onset of lower leaf concentric rings.
Tomato & Potato Late Blight Oomycete
Active: Cymoxanil 8% + Mancozeb 64% WP @ 1.5–2.0 g/L
Ensure complete canopy coverage; apply prophylactically before humid rainy spells.
Apple Scab (Venturia inaequalis) Orchard
Active: Difenoconazole 25% EC @ 0.3 ml/L or Captan 50% WP @ 2.5 g/L
Strictly respect pre-harvest intervals and rotate chemical groups to prevent resistance.

Crop & Fertilizer Advisor

Growth Stage N-P-K Principles
🌱 Primary Nutrient Functions:
  • Nitrogen (N): Stimulates chlorophyll production and vegetative foliage. Avoid over-application during flowering.
  • Phosphorus (P): Drives deep root establishment and cellular energy transfer during early crop establishment.
  • Potassium (K): Strengthens vascular xylem walls, regulates stomatal water loss, and boosts immune defense against leaf pathogens.
