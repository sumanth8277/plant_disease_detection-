🌾 Agronomic Crop Nutrition & Growth-Stage Principles

Fertilizer requirements are dynamic and depend on the specific crop variety, native soil fertility, pH balance, and vegetative versus reproductive growth stages:

  • Nitrogen (N): Essential for vegetative growth, canopy development, and leaf chlorophyll biosynthesis.
  • Phosphorus (P): Drives primary root architecture, cellular ATP energy transfer, and early flower induction.
  • Potassium (K): Regulates stomatal water conductance, drought tolerance, and natural disease resistance.
